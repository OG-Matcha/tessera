# Support

| You want to | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Ask how to do something, or share a setup | [Discussions](https://github.com/OG-Matcha/tessera/discussions) |
| Report something broken | [Bug report](https://github.com/OG-Matcha/tessera/issues/new?template=bug.yml) |
| Suggest a feature | [Feature request](https://github.com/OG-Matcha/tessera/issues/new?template=feature.yml) |
| Report a vulnerability | [Private advisory](https://github.com/OG-Matcha/tessera/security/advisories/new), see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |

English and Chinese are both fine. A bug report is quickest to fix with your terminal, your OS and the output of `claude --version`.
